## The Claim

> **ADD, SCALE, and SIGN are the floor every programming language
> eventually returns to** — the three irreducible operations underneath
> any computation, regardless of which language expresses it.
>
> This is the dividing line between **programming** (language-independent
> — reasoning at the level of ADD/SCALE/SIGN themselves) and **coding**
> (language-dependent — everything built on top of that floor in a
> specific syntax).

No mathematics or physics framing is intended for this paper by design —
these are presented as the prime operators of computation itself, in
computer-science terms only, deliberately independent of whatever deeper
structure they may also describe elsewhere.
