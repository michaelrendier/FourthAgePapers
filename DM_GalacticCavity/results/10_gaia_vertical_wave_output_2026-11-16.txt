PART D  period bound: an oscillation of amplitude A and period P has peak vertical speed 2 pi A / P; P >= 2 pi A / v_max
  A = 0.1 kpc (the z range seen across azimuth above)  v_max = 5.8 km/s  ->  P >= 1.07e+08 years
  A = 1 kpc (an undulation of kiloparsec amplitude)    v_max = 5.8 km/s  ->  P >= 1.07e+09 years
  matter following a wave of period lambda/c = 1e+05 yr (lambda = 100,000 ly) with A = 0.1 kpc would move vertically at 6.14e+03 km/s (0.02 of the speed of light)
  matter following a wave of period lambda/c = 1e+05 yr (lambda = 100,000 ly) with A = 1.0 kpc would move vertically at 6.14e+04 km/s (0.20 of the speed of light)
  so the measured vertical motion of the plane is >= 3 orders of magnitude slower than a c-speed wave of galactic wavelength; the observed plane does not
  oscillate with the wave. A period of ~1e9 years (the original draft) is consistent with the warp for kpc amplitude, and is not lambda/c.
