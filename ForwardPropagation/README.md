# ForwardPropagation: literature copies

Copies of the cited works that could be fetched for the paper *Forward Propagation of Information: Teaching The Maths how to speak English* (the paper, its generator and notebooks live in `VAPMIP/paper/` and `VAPMIP/notebooks/`). Each file was fetched with a Mozilla user agent and checked against its title page. The ledger is `ContextPlease/claude/references/INDEX.md`; works that could not be fetched are marked there and in the paper's References.

`references/`: Altman 2025; You (Epoch AI) 2025; Vahdat et al. (Google) 2025; de Marrais 2000; Moreno 1998; Corless et al. 1996; Wankel 1961 (US 2,988,065).
