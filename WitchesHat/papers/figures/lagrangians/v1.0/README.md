# The Lagrangians on the Mexican hat (SVG), version 1.0

Version 1.0 draws every path ON the potential surface V (2026-11-19, first pass). Version 2.0 (`../v2.0/`) draws what the maths gives for the whole bundle from the brim, with the action cone. Both are kept.

Generated by `../../../../simulator/render_lagrangian_svgs.py` (ValaQuenta venv; engines `witches_hat/action.py`, `lagrangian_sm`). One SVG per Lagrangian, four panels each: isometric, top, front, side. Brim in gold, hilltop in white, inflection circle r = 1/√3 dashed. Pictures are normalised to brim radius 1 and depth 1; the vertical axis is the potential energy V(q) at the path's position. The surface is cut away in the wedge (or half) facing the camera so the hill and the trough are visible; white curves in the front and side views are the section through the hilltop.

Limits: surface occlusion is a painter's-algorithm sort, so thin lines that pass behind a wall can show through in the isometric view; the tilt g = 0.2 of the VAPMIP hat is large compared with the depth, so the tilt is exaggerated relative to a physical coupling; the finite-temperature curves use the high-temperature expansion (valid above about 100 GeV) and show a weak first-order jump near 164 GeV where lattice calculations find a smooth crossover.
